# Hedge-Fund Review

## Workflow
1. Establish fund, investor/series/share class, valuation date and governing documents. Record side-letter precedence and effective dates.
2. For fees capture hurdle, high-water mark, equalization, crystallization, subscriptions/redemptions and loss carryforward rules. Send complete terms to an approved contractual calculator.
3. For NAV reconcile positions, price sources, cash, liabilities and capital activity at one declared timestamp. Flag stale marks and incomplete coverage independently of total reconciliation.
4. For regulatory preparation map fields to source evidence and applicable reporting instructions. Separate interpretation questions from deterministic checks.
5. Retain investor-level results, unresolved terms and independent review. Existing local benchmarks do not establish contractual accuracy for a real fund.

## Stopping Points
No production fee/NAV calculator or filing integration is supplied. Stop for missing legal terms, historical investor state, prices or tool access. Do not certify filings or authorize fees/distributions.

## Output
Term ledger, reconciliation findings, computed values only from approved executed tools, source evidence, and review requirements.
