# Private Credit And Fund Operations

## Workflow
1. Identify agreement, borrower/fund, reporting period, currency and authorized amendments. Extract definitions, exclusions, dates and rounding rules with source references.
2. For covenants distinguish maintenance/incurrence tests, EBITDA adjustments, cures and thresholds. For debt service specify accrual, day count, payment dates, rate resets, sweeps and priority.
3. Use approved calculators with explicit assumptions; do not infer missing agreement terms. Verify complete input periods and counterparties.
4. Apply distribution-reconciliation-review for conservation and completeness where appropriate. It does not determine contractual entitlement or validate a debt schedule.
5. Reconcile cash and investor reporting to source ledgers, retaining query IDs, policy exceptions and reviewer decisions.

## Stopping Points
Agreement-specific debt/covenant tools and source integrations are not supplied. Stop on missing terms, conflicting amendments or unsupported calculations. No payments, covenant waivers or investor-report publication.

## Output
Agreement-scoped term and exception ledger, actual deterministic results, evidence references and unresolved approvals.
