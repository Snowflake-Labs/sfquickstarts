Fraud-ring detection and investigation using relationship intelligence.

1. Fraud-ring and mule-network detection — best quick-start wedge

Business problem: Detect coordinated fraud that is invisible when each customer, account, or transaction is examined independently.

Relationship pattern:





Shared devices, phones, addresses, emails, identities, or bank accounts



Common beneficiaries, merchants, service providers, or intermediaries



Rapid fan-in/fan-out transaction patterns



Circular flows and pass-through accounts



Second- and third-degree connections to known fraudulent entities

RelationalAI contribution:





Build heterogeneous entity and transaction networks



Detect communities and suspicious motifs



Find similarity to known fraud rings



Propagate risk from confirmed cases through relationship paths



Produce investigator-ready explanations, not just a black-box score

This has credible customer resonance: Snowflake materials cite fraud networks as a graph use case, while RelationalAI examples describe detecting fraud rings and second- and third-degree connections. RelationalAI GA announcement

Best MVP: Start with payment or claims fraud and generate a ranked list of suspicious communities, connected entities, relationship paths, and estimated at-risk value.
